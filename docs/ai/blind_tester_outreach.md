# Blind and low-vision tester outreach (Muiz)

Rule from the design doc: if no tester is found, the usability test is **planned**, never **done**.

## Who to contact (start today, Day 3 is the session)
- Your university's disability support or student welfare office
- Local associations and clubs for blind and low-vision people
- Community groups where members use screen readers or voice assistants daily
- Friends-of-friends; ask for one or two people, not a crowd

## Message (edit names and dates)
> Hello, I am Muiz, a student at UNILAG, on Team Forte. For the Access Youth Tech Forge hackathon we are
> building Avenza, a voice layer for banking: you speak a transfer, the app reads back the bank-verified
> name and amount, and only your fingerprint approves it. We want to test it with people who use voice
> and screen readers every day, because they will find problems we cannot.
>
> We are asking for **20 to 30 minutes** on **Wednesday 11 November** (in person or by call). It is a
> test of our prototype, **not real money**: the bank is simulated. You can stop at any time, and we will not
> record your voice without your written permission. We are happy to share what we learn.
>
> Would you, or someone you know, be willing? Reply with a time that suits you.

## Session plan (20 to 30 minutes)
1. Explain: prototype, simulated bank, no real money, you may stop at any time. Ask consent for any recording.
2. Task 1: send a small amount to a named person. Task 2: cancel halfway by voice. Task 3: say something unclear
   and see how the app reacts.
3. Observe, do not help. Note where they hesitate, what they ask, and whether they trust what they heard.
4. Ask: Was the read-back clear? Too long? Did you know money had not moved when we said so? What would you change?
